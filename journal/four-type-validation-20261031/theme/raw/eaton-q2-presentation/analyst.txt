Highlights
Adjusted earnings per share of $3.15 and segment margins of 23.1% were both above the high end of our
guidance ranges of $3.00 to $3.10 and 22.6% to 23.0%, respectively.
Electrical Americas organic growth acceleration to 18% and margin expansion of 190 basis points over
prior quarter underscores execution momentum during capacity ramp.
On a rolling 12-month basis, strong organic order growth in Electrical Americas up 41%, Electrical
Global up 33%, and Aerospace up 17%.
Total backlog growth of 33% in Electrical Americas, 103% in Electrical Global and 28% in Aerospace.
In Q2, Electrical Sector data center orders are up approximately 85% and revenue up approximately 65%
versus 2Q25 demonstrating continued strong momentum in this rapid growth market.
Raising 2026 organic growth and adjusted EPS guidance.
