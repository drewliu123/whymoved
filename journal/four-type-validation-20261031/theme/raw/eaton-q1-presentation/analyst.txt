Highlights
On a rolling 12-month basis, organic order acceleration in Electrical Americas up 42% and strong
Electrical Global and Aerospace order growth both up 13%.
Total backlog growth of 44% in Electrical Americas, 73% in Electrical Global and 28% in Aerospace.
In Q1, Electrical Americas data center orders are up approximately 240% and revenue up approximately
50% versus 1Q25 demonstrating continued strong momentum in this rapid growth market.
Adjusted earnings per share of $2.81 in Q1, with segment margins of 22.7% above the high end of the
22.2 – 22.6% guidance range on 17% total revenue growth.
Bold moves in data center market with closing of Boyd Thermal and debut of Eaton Beam Rubin DSX
platform in collaboration with NVIDIA. Increased presence in defense aerospace with closing of Ultra PCS.
Raising 2026 organic growth guidance to 10% from 8% at the midpoint
