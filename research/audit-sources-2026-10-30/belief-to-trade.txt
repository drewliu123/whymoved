# Title:Beyond Forecasting: The Belief-to-Trade Layer in Prediction-Market Agents

Authors:[Yishu Wang](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Wang,+Y), [Yuxuan Wang](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Wang,+Y), [Jiaqi Deng](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Deng,+J), [Hanyang Tang](https://arxiv.org/search/cs?searchtype=author&query=Tang,+H)

View a PDF of the paper titled Beyond Forecasting: The Belief-to-Trade Layer in Prediction-Market Agents, by Yishu Wang and 3 other authors

[View PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03015)[HTML (experimental)](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.03015v1)
> Abstract:Forecasting future events has attracted growing attention as a testbed for general-purpose AI. A natural way to ground this evaluation is let the models trade in the prediction markets. Trading, however, requires more than forecasting. Moreover, recent benchmarks report a substantial gap between calibrated probability scores and the trading results. We propose Raven-Agent, to the best of our knowledge, the first autonomous trading agent for prediction markets. On a controlled replay over an archived decision set, our architecture achieves the only positive return and the only positive risk-adjusted return among all tested policies. We have released our code in [this https URL](https://github.com/Alchemist-X/predict-raven).
